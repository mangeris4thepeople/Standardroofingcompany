North of Fort Collins, the wind decides what lasts

Wellington's open exposure to north and west winds tears off ridge caps, lifts shingles, and finds every missed nail. Hail comes through too. We install for the exposure: six-nail high-wind patterns, sealed ridges, standing seam metal where it makes sense, and Class 4 shingles everywhere else.

Permits and code in Wellington and Larimer County

Reroofs inside the Town of Wellington are permitted through the town. Addresses outside town limits fall under Larimer County, which requires a Class A roof covering countywide, Class 4 impact-rated asphalt shingles on new and replacement roofs, an ice barrier from the eaves to at least two feet inside the exterior wall, and a roofing certification form. We confirm your jurisdiction and handle the paperwork.

Local Conditions

What Wellington roofs are up against

Wind

Sustained winds and gusts off the Wyoming border strip under-nailed roofs. Everything we install is rated and fastened for it.

Hail

Northern Larimer County sees regular hail. Class 4 shingles and metal are the long-term answers, and Larimer County requires Class 4 on replacement asphalt roofs.

Agricultural and shop buildings

Metal panel roofs on barns, shops, and outbuildings, repaired or replaced with proper fastening and trim.

Wellington roofing questions

Do you roof barns, shops, and outbuildings in Wellington?

Yes. Metal panel installation, repair, and replacement on agricultural and shop buildings is part of our regular work.

Does Larimer County require Class 4 shingles outside Wellington town limits?

Yes. Larimer County requires Class 4 impact-rated asphalt shingles and a Class A covering on new and replacement roofs, with an ice barrier and a signed roofing certification.
