Roof Replacement

Complete tear-off and rebuild, specified for Colorado hail, wind, and snow. You get an itemized scope before any work starts.

Request an estimate
  • Full tear-off to the deck. We inspect and replace damaged or rotted decking, priced per sheet in writing up front.
  • Ice and water shield at eaves, valleys, and penetrations per code and manufacturer requirements.
  • Synthetic underlayment, new drip edge, and new flashing. No reusing old metal where it should be replaced.
  • Ventilation balanced intake to exhaust to protect shingles and prevent ice dams.
  • Shingle options: Class 4 impact-resistant, architectural, designer, and standing seam metal.
  • Daily cleanup with magnetic nail sweeps and a final property walk.

Roof Repair

Leaks get worse every storm. We trace the water to its actual source and fix it, with before and after photos.

Request an estimate
  • Leak diagnosis at chimneys, skylights, valleys, vents, and pipe boots.
  • Wind damage: lifted, creased, and missing shingles replaced and sealed.
  • Flashing repair at walls, chimneys, and roof-to-wall transitions.
  • Emergency tarping after storms to stop interior damage.
  • Honest call: if a repair is the right answer, we will not sell you a roof.
