Our Story

Roofing done like real construction

Most roofing companies are sales operations with subcontracted crews. Standard Roofing runs every roof like a construction project: a written scope, a real schedule, one accountable project lead, and photos at every stage.

Nothing gets covered up until it is inspected, documented, and right. Schedules are real. Safety is not optional. Closeout means paperwork and photos, not just a handshake. That is how we build roofs.

We are a Loveland company, and we are invested in this community for the long haul.
