Local Conditions

What Greeley roofs are up against

Hail

Weld County regularly leads Colorado in hail reports. Class 4 shingles, metal, and Class 4 synthetic are the right long-term choices for Greeley homes.

Sun and heat

Open exposure and summer heat age asphalt and single-ply membranes. Reflective coatings and cool-roof membranes make a measurable difference on commercial buildings.

Wind

Plains wind drives rain under lifted shingles and strips loose membrane. We seal and fasten for the exposure.
