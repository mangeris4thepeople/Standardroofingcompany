Timnath, Larimer County

New homes, big hail, and HOA guidelines

Timnath is one of the newest communities in Northern Colorado, and nearly every roof in town is first-generation builder grade. When hail comes, those roofs are the first to go. We replace them with Class 4 shingles, stone-coated steel, and synthetic systems that satisfy your HOA's architectural guidelines and your insurer's discount program.

Permits and code in Timnath

Reroofs in the Town of Timnath are permitted through the town's Building Department and closed with a final inspection. Many Timnath neighborhoods also require architectural review for roof color and profile changes. We pull the permit, bring the HOA submittal, and give you the closed inspection record.

Local Conditions

What Timnath roofs are up against

Hail

Timnath sits in the same hail track that drove Fort Collins to mandate Class 4 shingles. We recommend Class 4 or better on every roof here.

Wind

Open exposure east of I-25 lifts shingles that were nailed for a calmer climate. High-wind installation is standard.

HOA review

Timnath Ranch, WildWing, Harmony Club, and others review roof materials. We provide samples and product sheets.

Timnath roofing questions

Will my Timnath HOA approve a metal or synthetic roof?

Most Timnath HOAs approve stone-coated steel and synthetic slate or shake in approved colors, and some approve standing seam. We check your guidelines and prepare the submittal.

Do you offer Class 4 shingles in Timnath?

Yes, as our standard recommendation. Many insurers discount them, and they are required next door in Fort Collins.
