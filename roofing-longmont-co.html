Longmont, Boulder and Weld Counties

Where the hail belt meets wildfire country

Longmont homes face Front Range hail from the east and, on the west side toward Lyons and Hygiene, wildfire resiliency rules that favor Class A roofs. Many older Longmont neighborhoods still carry cedar shake. We help homeowners choose roofs that satisfy both: Class 4 for hail, Class A for fire, and a look the HOA will approve.

Permits and code in Longmont

Reroofs in the City of Longmont are permitted through the city's Building Inspection Division and closed with a final inspection. Addresses in unincorporated Boulder County, Mead, Erie, Firestone, and Frederick each permit separately, and Boulder County restricts wood shake roofs in its wildfire zones. We confirm the jurisdiction and its requirements for your address before we quote.

Local Conditions

What Longmont roofs are up against

Hail

The St. Vrain Valley takes regular spring and summer hail. Class 4 shingles, metal, and Class 4 synthetic are the long-term fixes, and many carriers discount them.

Wildfire

West Longmont, Lyons, Hygiene, and the foothills fall into WUI zoning where Colorado's Wildfire Resiliency Code and Boulder County rules call for Class A coverings.

Wind and snow

Chinook winds and upslope snows load roofs along the foothills. Wind-rated installation and ice and water shield are standard.

Longmont roofing questions

Can I replace my cedar shake roof in Longmont with cedar again?

It depends on your address. Boulder County restricts wood shakes in wildfire zones and the state Wildfire Resiliency Code requires Class A coverings in designated WUI areas. In most cases we recommend Class A synthetic shake, which keeps the look and resolves insurance concerns.

Do you serve Erie, Firestone, and Frederick?

Yes. The Carbon Valley towns are within our regular Longmont service area.
