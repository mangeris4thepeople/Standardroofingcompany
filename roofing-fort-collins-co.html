Fort Collins, Larimer County

Fort Collins has the strictest roofing rules in Northern Colorado. Good.

Fort Collins requires UL 2218 Class 4 impact-resistant shingles on every asphalt roof, a rule that has been in place since January 2019, and every roofing permit must be closed with a waste management plan showing where the tear-off went. Most storm chasers learn this at the permit counter. We build to it on day one, and we recycle what can be recycled.

Permits and code in Fort Collins

Every Fort Collins reroof needs a building permit. Asphalt shingles must be UL 2218 Class 4 rated, and the manufacturer's documentation has to be available at inspection. The permit cannot be closed without a final waste management plan that lists the amount of material disposed, where it went, and the disposal and recycling tickets. Reroofs on properties in historic districts or on designated landmarks may need a certificate of appropriateness from Historic Preservation first. We manage all of it and give you the closed permit and the tickets with your closeout package.

Local Conditions

What Fort Collins roofs are up against

Hail

Fort Collins has taken multiple major hail events in the last decade, which is why the city mandated Class 4. Many insurers discount Class 4 roofs; we supply the documentation for your agent.

Wind

Foothill winds through Horsetooth and the west side of town routinely exceed the ratings on older three-tab shingles. We use six-nail high-wind installation on every roof.

Snow and ice

Older Old Town homes with low attic ventilation are ice dam territory. Balanced ventilation and ice and water shield at eaves are part of every replacement.

FAQ

Fort Collins roofing questions

Does Fort Collins require Class 4 shingles?

Yes. Since January 14, 2019, asphalt shingles installed in Fort Collins must be UL 2218 Class 4 impact-resistant. We install Class 4 as standard and keep the manufacturer documentation for inspection.

What is the Fort Collins roofing waste management plan?

A city requirement on every roofing permit. The contractor must document how much tear-off material was disposed of, where it went, and keep the landfill and recycling tickets. The permit cannot be closed without it. We handle it and give you copies.

Do you work in Fort Collins historic districts?

Yes. Reroofs on designated properties can require a certificate of appropriateness from the city's Historic Preservation office. We coordinate that review and match materials appropriately.

How far is Fort Collins from your office?

About 15 minutes. We are based in Loveland and work in Fort Collins every week.
