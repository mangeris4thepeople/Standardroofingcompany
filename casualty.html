Practice Areas

Where we can help your case

Opinions grounded in how buildings actually get built, inspected, and closed out, from someone who has run the job site.

Property & Hail Loss

Hail and wind damage verification, functional versus cosmetic damage, date-of-loss correlation with historical storm data, and scope disputes.

Cause & Origin of Roof Failure

Leaks, blow-offs, collapse, ice dams, and water intrusion. Was it weather, wear, defective installation, or defective material?

Construction Defect

Roofing, flashing, envelope, and workmanship defects on residential, multifamily, and commercial buildings, measured against code and manufacturer specifications.

Jobsite Injury & Falls

Fall protection, site safety planning, and superintendent and contractor standard of care review for jobsite casualty matters, measured against OSHA requirements and industry practice.

Repair Cost & Scope

Independent, line-item repair and replacement estimates, review of carrier and contractor scopes, and code upgrade analysis.

Appraisal & Bad Faith Support

Damage documentation and scope analysis supporting appraisal, coverage disputes, and claim-handling litigation.

How an engagement works

Intake

Send the matter basics. We run a conflict check before reviewing anything substantive.

Scope

Short call to define questions, deadlines, and whether you need a consulting or testifying expert.

Inspect

Site inspection with chain-of-custody photo documentation, measurements, and sampling where appropriate.

Report

Written findings and opinions tied to code, specification, and industry standards. Formatted to your disclosure requirements.

Testify

Available for deposition, mediation, appraisal panels, arbitration, and trial.

What You Get

Deliverables built to hold up

  • Site inspection report with indexed, dated photographs and roof diagrams.
  • Standards analysis referencing the applicable building code, manufacturer installation instructions, and OSHA requirements.
  • Repair or replacement estimate in line-item format.
  • Rebuttal review of opposing expert reports and carrier scopes.
  • Clear, plain-language opinions a jury or appraisal panel can follow.

Independence

Our opinions follow the evidence. We do not take contingency fees on expert work, and a retained casualty engagement is kept separate from any roofing sales or construction work on the same property.

Engagements are on an hourly or flat-fee basis under a written retention agreement. Rate sheet and CV available on request.
